Voice Control
via ElevenLabs

Hands-free emergency guidance. AID-I listens while you help — it transcribes what you say to 911 for the protocol, and responds to navigation commands to control the app without touching the screen.

Core Principle — No AI Responses to You ElevenLabs is NOT a chatbot in AID-I. You are talking to 911, not the app. The app only listens passively to build the protocol report. Voice commands navigate the interface only — the app never interrupts, never speaks back, never competes with your real emergency conversation.
