AID-I is an AI-powered emergency response companion designed to assist first responders and bystanders during critical medical situations. It provides real-time GPS-based mapping of nearby emergency resources — including hospitals, AEDs, fire stations, and police — so the closest help is always visible at a glance. A built-in AED guide walks users through a step-by-step cardiac emergency workflow with clear, panic-proof instructions when every second counts. AID-I records and transcribes emergency conversations using ElevenLabs Scribe, then automatically generates structured ATMIST and ABCDE medical reports ready for paramedic handoff. Built for speed and clarity under pressure, AID-I keeps responders in control through voice commands, live transcription, and intelligent reporting — all within a single streamlined interface.
